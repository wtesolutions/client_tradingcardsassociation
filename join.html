Become a Founding Supporter.

The National Trading Card Association is being built to serve the hobby for generations to come. Founding Supporters are the collectors, businesses and industry leaders helping make that vision possible.

Your support will help establish NTCA programs, educational resources, industry initiatives, events and the infrastructure needed to build a strong national organization.

Founding Supporter recognition will be available only during the NTCA's inaugural year founding period.

Every Founding Supporter, regardless of level, will be permanently recognized as part of the NTCA Founding Class. Founding Support is not the same as annual membership. Every contribution, regardless of amount, will help build a sustainable organization that can serve the trading card community for years to come. Our goal is to build the organization over the next year and open up memberships and partnership opportunities.

Other ways to get involved

We will unveil our focus areas in phases as the National Trading Card Association continues to grow. Let us know if you are interested in serving on a future committee or in an advisory capacity. Once the initial development phases are complete, those who have expressed interest will receive information about more in-depth opportunities to become involved.

Committees and forums under consideration:

  • Collector Education
  • Hobby Standards & Ethics
  • Events & Convention
  • Local Card Shops
  • Advocacy
  • Hall of Fame & Hobby History
  • Marketing & Communications
  • Youth / New Collector Development
  • International Development
