Our focus

01

Community, Events & Advocacy

We connect collectors, dealers, creators, and enthusiasts through a unified network that fosters collaboration, shared passion, and a sense of belonging across the hobby. We support and promote events while advocating for the interests of the trading card community.

02

Hobby Promotion and Growth

We actively promote the trading card hobby to new and existing audiences through marketing, partnerships, and grassroots initiatives designed to expand participation and long-term interest.

03

Education & Collector Development

We will provide resources, tools, and learning opportunities that help collectors of all levels grow their knowledge, make informed decisions, and maximize enjoyment of the hobby.

04

Standards, Trust & Accreditation

We establish and uphold industry standards that promote transparency, authenticity, and ethical practices, while offering accreditation that builds confidence across collectors, dealers, and partners.
