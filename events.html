Events

More than a card show.

The National Trading Card Association Convention will be the association's premier annual gathering, bringing together collectors, businesses, industry leaders, educators, creators and organizations from across the trading card community.

The annual convention

01

A comprehensive showcase

Unlike a traditional trading card show centered primarily on buying, selling and trading, the convention will serve as a comprehensive showcase of the people, organizations, innovations and trends shaping the hobby — seminars, presentations, panel discussions, demonstrations and networking.

02

Hall of Fame & awards

The convention is intended to coincide with the annual National Trading Card Hall of Fame induction, an industry awards ceremony and a formal banquet — recognizing the individuals, organizations, achievements and historic contributions that have shaped the trading card community.

03

In person or online

Select seminars, presentations, interviews and ceremonies may be livestreamed or made available through digital programming, so collectors, speakers and industry representatives can take part regardless of location.
