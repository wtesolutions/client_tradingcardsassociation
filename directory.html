National Trading Card Association

One Association. One Community. A Shared Future.

The National Trading Card Association is the umbrella organization created to unite, support and advance every part of the trading card community. We bring together collectors, hobby shops, dealers, manufacturers, grading and authentication services, distributors, auction companies, content creators, event organizers and other industry partners through a shared commitment to the hobby's long-term success.

Trading cards are more than collectibles. They preserve history, celebrate achievement, connect generations and create communities around sports, entertainment, gaming and popular culture. As the industry continues to grow, the National Trading Card Association will provide a national platform for cooperation, education, ethical practices, advocacy and innovation.

Our vision

Our vision is to build a trusted, inclusive and connected trading card community in which collectors and industry participants can learn, collaborate and thrive. We seek to preserve the hobby's history while developing the programs, standards and relationships needed to protect and strengthen its future.

A network of specialized initiatives

The National Trading Card Association will serve as the foundation for a network of specialized initiatives, each addressing an important area of the hobby:

  • National Trading Card Hall of Fame — Honoring the individuals, organizations, brands, innovations and historic moments that have shaped the trading card community.
  • National Trading Card Convention — Bringing collectors, businesses, creators and industry leaders together for education, networking, exhibits, recognition and the celebration of the hobby.
  • National Trading Card Foundation — Supporting education, youth engagement, charitable programs and the preservation of trading card history.
  • Advocacy Network — Giving the trading card community a more informed and coordinated voice on public policies and issues that may affect collectors and businesses.
  • Standards, Trust and Accreditation — Encouraging transparency, authenticity, consumer confidence and responsible practices throughout the marketplace.
  • Education and Collector Development — Providing useful information, tools and learning opportunities for collectors at every level of experience.
  • International Collaboration — Building relationships with collectors, organizations and industry partners around the world to encourage cooperation and responsible global growth.

Building together

These focus areas will be introduced in phases as the association develops its leadership, membership and programs. Each initiative will have a distinct purpose while remaining connected through the broader mission and values of the National Trading Card Association.

We believe the future of the hobby should be shaped by the people who participate in it. That is why we invite collectors, businesses, experts and enthusiasts from across the trading card community to share their knowledge and become involved. Opportunities to serve on committees and advisory groups will be announced as each phase is developed.

From local card shops and first-time collectors to established businesses and international partners, everyone has a role in the future of trading cards. Together, we can preserve the hobby's history, strengthen trust in its marketplace and build a more connected community for generations to come.

National Trading Card Foundation

Preserving the Hobby. Inspiring the Future.

The National Trading Card Foundation is the charitable and educational arm of the National Trading Card Association. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, the Foundation is dedicated to preserving the history of trading cards, expanding educational opportunities, engaging young collectors and using the hobby to make a positive difference in communities.

Trading cards have introduced generations to sports, history, art, entertainment, strategy and entrepreneurship. They encourage curiosity, strengthen personal connections and provide a meaningful way for people of different ages and backgrounds to share stories and experiences. The National Trading Card Foundation will build upon these qualities by creating programs that make collecting more accessible, educational and inclusive.

Our vision

Our vision is to ensure that the history, knowledge and enjoyment of trading cards are preserved and shared with future generations. We seek to create opportunities for people to learn through collecting, participate in the hobby and experience the sense of connection that trading cards can provide.

Where the Foundation will focus

The Foundation will work alongside collectors, educators, community organizations and industry partners to develop charitable and educational initiatives in several areas:

  • Youth Engagement — Introducing young people to responsible collecting through educational programs, starter collections, community events and mentorship opportunities.
  • Education and Financial Literacy — Using trading cards to teach history, research, organization, budgeting, entrepreneurship and informed decision-making.
  • Historical Preservation — Protecting the stories, artifacts and records that document the development of sports, entertainment and trading card culture.
  • Community Outreach — Bringing the enjoyment of collecting to schools, libraries, hospitals, youth organizations and underserved communities.
  • Scholarships and Grants — Developing future opportunities that support education, research, preservation and community-based programs connected to the hobby.
  • Charitable Partnerships — Working with nonprofit organizations, collectors and industry participants to support worthwhile causes through donated cards, fundraising activities and special events.

Collecting with a purpose

The National Trading Card Foundation believes that the value of a card extends far beyond its market price. A card can help a student discover history, give a young person a sense of belonging, preserve the legacy of an important individual or create a lasting connection between generations.

Through thoughtful partnerships and responsible stewardship, the Foundation will help transform a shared passion for trading cards into meaningful educational and charitable opportunities. Its programs will be introduced in phases as relationships are established and resources become available.

We invite collectors, businesses, educators, historians and community partners to help shape this work. Opportunities to volunteer, contribute, serve in an advisory capacity and support specific programs will be announced as the Foundation grows.

Together, we can preserve the heritage of trading cards, open the hobby to new participants and demonstrate how collecting can educate, inspire and strengthen communities.

National Trading Card Convention

Connecting the Hobby. Showcasing the Industry. Shaping the Future.

The National Trading Card Convention will be the association's premier annual gathering, bringing together collectors, businesses, industry leaders, educators, creators and organizations from across the trading card community.

Unlike a traditional trading card show centered primarily on buying, selling and trading, the convention will serve as a comprehensive showcase of the people, organizations, innovations and trends shaping the hobby. Through seminars, presentations, panel discussions, demonstrations and networking opportunities, participants will gain a broader understanding of the trading card industry and its future.

Our vision

Our vision is to create an annual destination where every part of the trading card community can be represented, recognized and connected. The convention will provide a national platform for sharing knowledge, building relationships, celebrating achievement and encouraging responsible growth throughout the hobby.

Programming

Programming is expected to explore a wide range of subjects, including:

  • The history and preservation of trading cards
  • Emerging industry trends and technologies
  • Grading, authentication and counterfeit prevention
  • Collecting, storage, insurance and collection management
  • Manufacturing, licensing and distribution
  • Local card shops and small-business development
  • Auctions, marketplaces and consumer protection
  • Sports, entertainment and trading card games
  • Youth engagement and collector education
  • Content creation and community building
  • Responsible card purchasing and investing
  • The future of the global trading card industry

Collectors will have opportunities to share their experiences, interests and notable collections, while businesses and organizations can present new ideas, services and innovations. Industry experts, historians and experienced collectors will help provide balanced perspectives on both the traditions of the hobby and the opportunities ahead.

A celebration of the trading card community

The convention is intended to coincide with the annual National Trading Card Hall of Fame induction, an industry awards ceremony and a formal banquet. Together, these events will recognize the individuals, organizations, achievements and historic contributions that have helped shape the trading card community.

Participate in person or online

To make the convention accessible to the widest possible audience, opportunities will be available both in person and online. Select seminars, presentations, interviews and ceremonies may be livestreamed or made available through digital programming.

Virtual participation will allow collectors, speakers and industry representatives to take part regardless of location. Online exhibits, educational sessions and networking opportunities may also complement the in-person experience.

More than a card show

The convention will not be defined solely by transactions. It will be a place to learn, exchange ideas, recognize achievement and build lasting connections across the hobby.

Convention programming and participation opportunities will be introduced in phases. As plans develop, the National Trading Card Association will invite speakers, presenters, sponsors, exhibitors, volunteers and advisory participants to become involved.

By bringing together the history, people, businesses and emerging ideas of the trading card world, the convention will celebrate where the hobby has been while helping shape where it goes next.

National Trading Card Hall of Fame

Honoring the People and History of the Hobby.

The National Trading Card Hall of Fame honors the individuals, organizations, brands, innovations and historic moments that have shaped the trading card community.

It is the institution that preserves the legacy of the most influential figures, brands and moments in trading card history, recognized each year through a Hall of Fame induction and an industry awards ceremony.

What the Hall of Fame honors

  • Individuals
  • Organizations
  • Brands
  • Innovations
  • Historic moments

Induction, awards and banquet

The annual induction is intended to coincide with the National Trading Card Convention, alongside an industry awards ceremony and a formal banquet. Together, these events will recognize the individuals, organizations, achievements and historic contributions that have helped shape the trading card community.

The Hall of Fame induction will honor lasting influence and distinguished service to the hobby. The awards ceremony will celebrate excellence, innovation, leadership and community impact during the preceding year. The annual banquet will bring attendees together for an evening of recognition, fellowship and shared appreciation for trading cards.

National Trading Card Advocacy Network

A Unified Voice for the Trading Card Community.

The National Trading Card Advocacy Network will represent the interests of collectors, hobby shops, dealers, manufacturers, grading and authentication companies, distributors, auction houses, content creators, event organizers and other partners throughout the trading card community.

As the hobby and industry continue to grow, decisions made by lawmakers, regulators, sports organizations, entertainment companies and other stakeholders can have a significant impact on the people and businesses involved. Our goal is to ensure that the trading card community has an informed, organized and respected voice in those conversations.

Our vision

Our vision is to build a nationwide advocacy network that monitors important developments, educates decision-makers and advances policies that support a fair, transparent and thriving trading card marketplace.

Key areas of focus

  • National Lobby Days — Hosting organized advocacy days in Washington, D.C., where collectors, businesses and industry representatives can meet with elected officials and policymakers.
  • Nationwide Legislative Monitoring — Tracking federal and state legislation, regulations and policy proposals that could affect the trading card hobby and industry.
  • Partner Advocacy — Representing the interests and concerns of association members and partners while helping them better understand emerging public-policy issues.
  • Industry Representation — Serving as a voice to professional sports organizations, entertainment companies, licensing groups and other industries connected to trading cards.
  • Education and Awareness — Providing lawmakers, regulators and industry leaders with accurate information about the economic, cultural and community impact of trading cards.
  • Grassroots Engagement — Giving collectors, hobby shops and industry professionals opportunities to communicate with policymakers and participate in coordinated advocacy efforts.
  • Responsible Industry Growth — Supporting policies and partnerships that encourage innovation, consumer confidence, small-business development and the long-term growth of collecting.

Advocacy built on participation

Effective advocacy begins with listening. The National Trading Card Advocacy Network will seek input from collectors, businesses and industry partners to identify shared priorities and understand how proposed policies may affect different parts of the hobby.

Participation opportunities will be introduced in phases and may include advisory groups, legislative alerts, policy briefings, surveys, grassroots campaigns and national lobby days. Members and partners will be invited to share their experiences and help communicate the importance of the trading card industry to elected officials and other decision-makers.

Together, we can give the trading card community a stronger voice, advocate for those who contribute to its success and help create an environment in which the hobby and industry can continue to grow.

International Trading Card Association

Connecting the Global Trading Card Community.

The International Trading Card Association will be a sister organization of the National Trading Card Association, sharing a similar mission while serving collectors, businesses and industry partners on an international stage.

Trading cards cross borders, languages and cultures. Collectors around the world share an appreciation for sports, entertainment, gaming, history and the stories preserved through cards. As international participation continues to grow, the International Trading Card Association will help create stronger connections among collectors, hobby shops, manufacturers, grading and authentication services, distributors, marketplaces, event organizers and other industry stakeholders worldwide.

Our vision

Our vision is to build an inclusive international network that encourages cooperation, education, trust and responsible growth throughout the global trading card community.

The International Trading Card Association will provide a platform through which organizations and individuals from different countries can exchange ideas, identify shared priorities and address challenges affecting the hobby. Its work will respect the distinct laws, customs, languages and collecting traditions of each participating country while promoting common principles that strengthen the international marketplace.

Areas of focus

The organization's areas of focus are expected to include:

  • Global Collaboration — Connecting collectors, associations, businesses and industry leaders across national and regional markets.
  • Information and Resource Sharing — Providing educational materials, industry insights and practical guidance relevant to international collectors and businesses.
  • International Standards and Trust — Encouraging transparency, authenticity, consumer confidence and responsible practices across borders.
  • Education and Collector Development — Supporting learning opportunities for collectors of all ages and experience levels.
  • Industry Growth — Promoting responsible expansion, innovation and greater participation in established and emerging trading card markets.
  • Cultural and Historical Preservation — Recognizing the unique cards, traditions, individuals and milestones that have shaped collecting throughout the world.
  • International Events and Engagement — Creating opportunities for conferences, presentations, virtual programs and partnerships that bring the global community together.

A shared mission and partnership

The National Trading Card Association and the International Trading Card Association will remain distinct but closely connected sister organizations. A continuing collaboration of information, resources, research and guidance will allow both organizations to benefit from one another's knowledge and experience.

The National Trading Card Association will offer insights gained from its work within the United States, while the International Trading Card Association and its participating partners will provide valuable perspectives from markets and collecting communities around the world. This two-way exchange will help both organizations better understand developing trends, emerging concerns and new opportunities.

Joint educational programs, research initiatives, virtual presentations, industry discussions and special events may also be developed to encourage greater cooperation between national and international participants.

One hobby without borders

The International Trading Card Association will be developed in phases as relationships are established with collectors, businesses and organizations in different countries. Opportunities to serve as international representatives, advisers, regional leaders and program partners will be announced as the organization grows.

Regardless of language or location, trading cards bring people together through shared interests and memorable stories. By connecting communities, exchanging knowledge and building trusted international relationships, the International Trading Card Association will help strengthen the hobby at home and around the world.
