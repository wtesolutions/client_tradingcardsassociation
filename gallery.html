Photos from the community as the NTCA grows — collections, shops and gatherings. Drop your photography onto the slots below; images shot on dark backgrounds blend best into the page.

Community submissions

Send us a photo from your corner of the hobby.

Collections, shop visits, show floors, card-room setups, youth nights — anything that shows the hobby as it really looks. Every photo is reviewed by the NTCA before it appears on this page.

  1. 01

    You submit

    Up to five photos with a short caption and the name you would like the credit line to read.

  2. 02

    We review

    An NTCA volunteer checks each photo for rights, quality and content. Most submissions are cleared within a few days.

  3. 03

    It goes live

    Approved photos join the gallery above with your credit line. Ask us to pull yours at any time and it comes down.

    In review

    Thanks — your photos are in the queue.

    An NTCA volunteer reviews every submission before it is published. We will email you at the address you gave when a decision is made, usually within a few days.
